## House Fool

Nobody remembers who first bought you. It was a man who liked a quiet laugh at his table while his guests discussed things best not repeated. You learned the job quickly: be loud, be harmless, and be furniture. Men say anything in front of a fool, including the names of the people they intend to have killed. After enough dinners that ended with a body in the cellar, laughing became the only sane response. You never quite stopped. Silence has felt wrong ever since, because a room with no audience is a room where something is about to happen to you.

- +10 Speechcraft and Acrobatics
- For each non-hostile NPC nearby (up to 5): +3 Sneak, Speechcraft and Personality
- With nobody nearby: -10 Willpower and -10 Sneak

## Sugar-Paid

The troupe never paid in coin. They paid in moon sugar, because it was cheaper and because a fool who’s bright and fast on stage doesn’t ask about wages. The highs were for the audience, and the crashes you kept for yourself. A decade on, the troupe is long gone, but your body still runs on its schedule.

- Every 2 game days you swap between two moods:
    - Manic: +15 Personality, Agility and Speed, +10 Acrobatics, -15 Willpower and Intelligence
    - Hollow: +15 Willpower and Intelligence, +10 Mysticism, -15 Personality and Agility
- Eating moon sugar forces a Manic spell for the rest of the day, and the swap schedule then shifts accordingly.

## Keystone

When the magister built his summoning circle, he needed something to hold the bindings in place. He used you. Every creature he ever called passed through you first, and over the years you learned to let it, to be the thing the magic rests on while it does its work. It left you thin. Magic goes through you easily now, and so do blades. Whatever you are wearing, you can lend it to the things you call. You have never lent it back, because nothing you call is able to.

- +10 Conjuration and Willpower
- Any spells you cast on yourself are also applied to your active summons.
- +35% Weakness to Magicka, Elemental Magic and Physical Damage
